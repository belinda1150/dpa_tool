## Key Compliance References

### Zimbabwe Data Protection Requirements

**ROPA (Records of Processing Activities):**
- Article 14, Zimbabwe DPA
- See USER_MANUAL.md section 5.1
- Must be maintained and kept current

**DPIA (Data Protection Impact Assessment):**
- Required for high-risk processing
- See USER_MANUAL.md section 5.2
- DPO approval required

**Data Subject Rights:**
- 30-day response deadline
- See USER_MANUAL.md section 5.4
- Free of charge (first request)

**Data Breach Notification:**
- 72 hours to notify POTRAZ
- See USER_MANUAL.md section 5.5
- DPO must be informed immediately

**Cross-Border Transfers:**
- Adequate protection required
- See USER_MANUAL.md section 5.7
- POTRAZ approval if high-risk

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where do I start?**
A: Begin with QUICK_START_GUIDE.md (10 minutes), then refer to ROLE_GUIDE.md for your specific role.

**Q: I need detailed instructions for a specific task. Where do I look?**
A: USER_MANUAL.md has step-by-step procedures for all system functions. Use the table of contents to navigate.

**Q: I'm setting up the system for the first time. What do I read?**
A: ADMIN_SETUP_GUIDE.md walks you through installation, configuration, and go-live.

**Q: How do I know what I'm allowed to do in the system?**
A: ROLE_GUIDE.md has a permission matrix showing what each role can access. Also see USER_MANUAL.md § 3.

**Q: What's the difference between ROPA and DPIA?**
A: ROPA documents what you do with personal data. DPIA assesses privacy risks for high-risk processing. See USER_MANUAL.md § 5.1-5.2.

**Q: How quickly must we respond to a data subject request?**
A: 30 days from receipt (Zimbabwe DPA requirement). See USER_MANUAL.md § 5.4.

**Q: What triggers a DPIA requirement?**
A: Large-scale processing (>10,000 subjects), sensitive data, automated decisions, systematic monitoring. System checks automatically. See USER_MANUAL.md § 5.2.

**Q: How long do we have to notify POTRAZ about a data breach?**
A: 72 hours from discovery. See USER_MANUAL.md § 5.5 and ROLE_GUIDE.md DPO section.
